# Release Notes

## [v1.4.0] - 2026-01-31

### 🚀 New Features

- **Dividend Income Input**: Added a separate field for Dividend Income, distinct from other investment income.
- **Updated Tax Logic**:
  - **Dividend Income** is now correctly taxed at a final WHT of **15%**.
  - **Investment Income** (Interest / Fixed Deposits) remains at **10%**.
- **UI Improvements**: Updated input placeholders to reduced ambiguity ("Share Profits" for Dividends, "Interest / Fixed Deposits" for Investments).

### ⚙️ Improvements

- **Compliance**: Fully aligned with **Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 02 of 2025** regarding the separation of dividend and interest tax rates for Y/A 2025/2026.
